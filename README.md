**Privacy Policy**

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

Zipline is a number-path puzzle for Android. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what it sends to Google when ads and analytics are on.

**Who we are**

Zipline is published by Lazy Developer Products. Questions about this policy: **[your email]**.

**Information the app keeps on your device**

Play, puzzles, and the daily puzzle work with no account and no network. The app stores this on the phone:

- Settings, including theme, accent, timer, move counter, error highlighting, sound, and vibration
- Level progress, stars, best times, and the last level you opened
- Daily streak and play history
- Hint balance
- How many puzzles you have solved, so the app can space full-screen ads
- Whether the Play review prompt has already been shown

Android backup can include this data if backup is turned on for the phone. Uninstalling Zipline removes the copy stored in the app.

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or age, and it does not upload puzzle solutions.

**Analytics**

Zipline uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. Events cover button taps and puzzle start, finish, restart, and leave, plus whether a step was correct. Each event can include puzzle type, grid size, difficulty, level number, attempt count, time, hints used, moves, stars, and how much of the grid was filled.

These events do not include your name, email, age, or the solution. Firebase may attach an app instance identifier and basic device and app information, as described in [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Advertising**

Zipline uses Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, the app can show a banner on Home, a full-screen ad after a run of solved puzzles, and a rewarded video that adds hints.

AdMob and related Google services may use the advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads. In regions where consent is required, Google’s consent form appears before ads are requested. Your choice is stored by that consent tool.

Google’s policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [AdMob and ads](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalization, in your device settings.

**Remote configuration**

Firebase Remote Config supplies ad settings, such as whether ads are on and how often a full-screen ad can appear. That request does not include puzzle solutions or an account.

**Permissions**

Zipline asks to vibrate for haptic feedback. The advertising and analytics libraries also use internet access, network state, and the advertising identifier so ads and analytics can run. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage.

**Children**

Zipline is a general-audience puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Sharing**

Zipline does not sell your information. The share action builds a message on your device with size, difficulty, time, moves, and stars. That message does not include the solution. It is sent only if you choose to share it through another app.

Google Play may also process the in-app review prompt under [Google Play’s terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and privacy policy.

**Retention**

Progress and settings stay on the device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Analytics and ad data are kept by Google under Google’s own retention rules.

**Changes**

If this policy changes, the new text will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
